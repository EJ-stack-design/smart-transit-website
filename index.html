WELCOME TO SMART TRANSIT

Making Public Transportation Smarter and Easier

Smart Transit is a mobile-based transportation monitoring system designed to help passengers access useful travel information. Through GPS-based vehicle tracking and mobile notifications, passengers can stay informed about their selected routes and transportation progress.

WHAT SMART TRANSIT OFFERS

Features Designed for Better Travel

Access helpful transportation information directly from your mobile device.

📍

Live Vehicle Tracking

View the current location of tracked public transportation vehicles on the map.

🗺️

Route Monitoring

Select your pickup and destination stops and monitor the progress of your journey.

⏱️

Estimated Arrival

Check estimated arrival information to help you plan your trip.

🔔

Arrival Notifications

Receive notifications when your selected vehicle approaches your pickup or destination.
