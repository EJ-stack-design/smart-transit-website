About Smart Transit

Making public transportation information more accessible through technology, GPS-based vehicle tracking, and mobile connectivity.

WHO WE ARE

A Smarter Way to Monitor Public Transportation

Smart Transit is a mobile-based public transportation monitoring system developed to help passengers access useful travel information.

The system uses GPS-based vehicle tracking and a mobile application to provide information about vehicle locations, selected routes, estimated arrival details, and notifications.

Through this platform, passengers can stay informed about their journeys and make better-informed travel decisions.

PROJECT BACKGROUND

Why We Developed Smart Transit

Providing passengers with easier access to transportation information.

Passengers often need to know where public transportation vehicles are and when they may arrive at their selected locations. Without accessible vehicle information, planning a trip can be difficult.

Smart Transit was developed to address this information gap through a technology-based transportation monitoring platform. By integrating vehicle GPS data, a backend system, and a mobile application, the platform aims to make relevant transportation information more accessible to passengers.
