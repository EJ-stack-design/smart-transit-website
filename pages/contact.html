Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about Smart Transit.

What is Smart Transit?

Smart Transit is a mobile-based public transportation monitoring system that provides vehicle tracking, route monitoring, estimated arrival information, and notifications.

Where can I download the application?

The application is planned for Android and iOS devices. Official download links will be provided once it is published.

View Download Information →

What route does Smart Transit support?

The system is designed around the Silago Terminal to Sogod Terminal route in Southern Leyte.

How can I report a problem or send feedback?

You may contact the Smart Transit team through our official email address or phone number listed on this page.

How can I learn to use the application?

You can visit the User Manual page for instructions and information about the application's features.
