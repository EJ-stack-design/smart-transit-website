01

Introduction to Smart Transit

Smart Transit is a mobile-based public transportation monitoring system designed to help passengers access useful travel information.

Through GPS-based vehicle tracking and mobile notifications, passengers can monitor transportation vehicles, select their preferred pickup and destination locations, and stay informed about their journey.

Purpose of the Application

To provide passengers with accessible transportation information and help them make more informed travel decisions.

02

Application Installation

Smart Transit is a passenger mobile application. The Android release will be distributed through the official Smart Transit website as an APK file. iOS availability will be announced separately.

For Android Users

  1. Visit the official Smart Transit website.
  2. Open the Download App page.
  3. Tap the Android APK download button once the official release is available.
  4. Wait for the APK file to finish downloading.
  5. Open the downloaded APK file.
  6. If Android requests permission to install apps from your browser or file manager, review the prompt and allow it only for this trusted installation.
  7. Follow the installation instructions displayed on your smartphone.
  8. Open the Smart Transit application.

For iOS Users

The Smart Transit iOS application is not currently available. Please check the official website for future availability updates.

03

Getting Started

After installing Smart Transit, open the application on your smartphone to access its available passenger features.

Basic Steps

  1. Launch the Smart Transit application.
  2. Open the passenger interface.
  3. Navigate to the route selection and vehicle monitoring features.
  4. Choose your pickup and destination locations to view relevant transportation information.
04

Selecting Your Pickup and Destination

Passengers can select their preferred pickup and destination locations to monitor a journey along the available route.

How to Select Your Route

  1. Open the route selection feature.
  2. Choose your pickup location.
  3. Choose your destination.
  4. Review your selected route.
  5. View the available vehicle and journey information.
Changing Your Travel Direction

If you change your selected travel direction, the previous pickup and destination selections are automatically reset. Select your locations again for the new direction.

05

Tracking Public Transportation Vehicles

The vehicle tracking feature allows passengers to view the current reported location of tracked vehicles on the map.

How to Track a Vehicle

  1. Open the vehicle tracking or map interface.
  2. Select your preferred pickup and destination locations.
  3. View the vehicle's reported location on the map.
  4. Monitor the vehicle's movement along its route.
Important Reminder

Vehicle tracking information depends on the availability of GPS data and the vehicle's connection to the system. Location updates may be delayed or temporarily unavailable.

06

Estimated Arrival Information

Smart Transit provides estimated arrival information to help passengers understand the vehicle's expected progress toward their selected locations.

How to Check Arrival Information

  1. Select your pickup and destination.
  2. Open the vehicle or route information displayed in the application.
  3. Check the estimated arrival information available for your selected locations.
  4. Continue monitoring the vehicle as it approaches your stops.
Note About Estimated Arrival

Estimated arrival information is based on available vehicle location and route data. Actual arrival times may change because of traffic, road conditions, or GPS update delays.

07

Arrival Notifications

Smart Transit provides notifications to inform passengers when a tracked vehicle approaches or reaches their selected pickup and destination locations.

Notification Events

📍
Approaching Location

A notification is triggered when the vehicle is approximately 500 meters from a selected location.

🔔
Arrived at Location

A notification is triggered when the vehicle is approximately 100 meters from a selected location.

Notification Reminder

Notifications depend on available GPS updates, system connectivity, and notification permissions on your smartphone. Keep notifications enabled to receive available alerts.

08

Troubleshooting

Below are some common situations that passengers may encounter while using Smart Transit.

Vehicle location is not updating

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Refresh or reopen the application.
  • Check again after the vehicle's GPS data becomes available.

Estimated arrival information is unavailable

  • Check whether a pickup and destination have been selected.
  • Check whether vehicle location data is available.
  • Try again when the system receives updated vehicle information.

Notifications are not received

  • Check the notification permissions in your smartphone settings.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure the selected route and locations are correct.

Pickup and destination selections are reset

  • This may happen when the travel direction is changed.
  • Select your pickup and destination again for the new direction.
